Davanagere Villa Restoration

Transforming heritage into contemporary elegance

About This Project

The restoration of the old DVG villa represents a harmonious blend of preservation and innovation. This historic property, nestled in Davangere, has been meticulously transformed into a chic contemporary residence while maintaining its timeless architectural charm.

Our approach focused on celebrating the villa's original character—its grand proportions, classical details, and spatial flow—while introducing modern amenities and design elements that meet contemporary living standards. Every corner tells a story of careful restoration, from the preserved original flooring to the reimagined living spaces that now flow seamlessly with natural light.

The project exemplifies our philosophy of responsive architecture, where historical context informs modern design. By honoring the villa's heritage while embracing contemporary aesthetics, we've created a space that transcends time, offering both nostalgia and modern comfort.

Location
Davangere, Karnataka

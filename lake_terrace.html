Lake Terrace

A thoughtfully designed luxury reverse duplex at Embassy that blends contemporary living with traditional warmth through fluid planning, rich textures, and cohesive elegance.

About This Project

This luxury reverse duplex located in Embassy, at the heart of the city is thoughtfully designed for a modern family of four, where contemporary living is seamlessly rooted in tradition. The home unfolds with fluid spatial planning, offering privacy and openness in equal measure, while each level is carefully curated to suit evolving family needs. Distinct themes define every room—ranging from warm traditional accents to refined modern expressions—yet remain cohesively tied through a shared palette and material language.

Rich textures, crafted details, and layered lighting elevate the everyday experience, creating a home that celebrates individuality, cultural grounding, and understated luxury in perfect harmony.

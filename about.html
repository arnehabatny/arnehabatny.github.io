About Us

At the heart of Belaku's philosophy is the idea that architecture should respond to nature, rather than compete with it. The studio's name, Belaku, means "light" in Kannada, symbolizing the studio's commitment to harnessing the power of daylight in all its forms. The team believes that the simple act of letting in more natural light and air can transform a space—improving mood, increasing productivity, and fostering a deeper connection between people and their environment.

Meet the Principal Architect

Ar. Neha Batny

Principal Architect & Founder

Architect Neha is a design-driven professional with a Master's degree in Architectural Design from the University of Sheffield. She has handled over 20+ projects across Bangalore, Davangere, Chennai, and Mysuru, with a strong focus on premium residential projects and adaptive reuse.

Her work thoughtfully blends contemporary design sensibilities with contextual sensitivity, creating spaces that are refined, functional, and enduring.

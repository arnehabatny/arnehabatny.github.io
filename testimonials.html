Seeguvani Residence

Building a home that reflects our roots has always been a dream. Being from Chikmagalur, we wanted our house to carry the essence of our hometown, especially Vivek's (my husband's) native village, Seeguvani, which also inspired the name of our home.

From the start, we envisioned a space filled with natural light, good ventilation, and a strong connection to nature. Our architect, Neha, brought this vision to life with great sensitivity, ensuring these elements were never compromised.

The house balances functionality and aesthetics while thoughtfully following Vastu, creating a calm and positive energy throughout. Features like the skylight and wooden staircase add warmth and character, while materials such as teakwood, terracotta jaali bricks, and Mangalore tiles give the home an earthy feel. The garden spaces and the Tulasi Katte strengthen our connection to the land, while a bullock cart wheel stands as a subtle symbol of our agricultural roots.

One of our favourite corners is the French garden door that opens into a quiet green space—perfect for a cup of coffee and unhurried conversations. Every detail feels intentional and rooted.

Even though this was only Neha's second major project, her maturity, dedication, and clarity stood out from day one. She coordinated seamlessly with multiple teams, patiently accommodated our ideas and changes, and stayed committed until we were fully satisfied.

We are truly grateful to Neha for shaping Seeguvani, our home that carries our roots, stories, heart, and the many dreams we look forward to building within it.

— Sudeeksha
